About the group

The group was founded in May 2026 by five people.

It operates at the University of Warsaw and is university-wide.

Activities

Proposed amendments to the draft resolution on artificial intelligence in teaching

Aleksander Rej prepared and published under the group’s banner 13 proposed amendments and 3 analytical threads on the draft resolution of the University Council for Education (Uniwersytecka Rada ds. Kształcenia, URK) on artificial intelligence in teaching.

The website arejgh.github.io/propozycje-urk-uw presents the proposals together with their justification and sources.

Resolution No. 75

Resolution No. 75 of the URK (Uchwała nr 75 URK) of 11 September 2026 on guidelines for the use of artificial intelligence tools in teaching has been in force since 1 October 2026.

The proposals website compares each of the 13 proposals with the text of the adopted resolution. The comparison sets the wording of both documents side by side and quotes them verbatim.

Computational social science

The group is developing computational social science as an area of research and projects that combine the methods of the social sciences with large language models and AI agents.

Membership and cooperation

Recruitment to the group is openRecruitment. The group is recruiting. Applications can be sent through the application form or by email to insai@uw.edu.pl. Under the regulations, membership begins after a three-month probationary period, counted from the board resolution that accepts the written membership declaration.Fill in the form

Membership. Any student of the University of Warsaw can become a member of the group; doctoral candidates of the University of Warsaw who demonstrate an interest in pursuing the group’s goals may also apply for membership.

Sections and teams. Under the regulations, thematic sections and project teams, set up by resolution of the board, may operate within the group.

Cooperation. The regulations provide for cooperation with University of Warsaw units, student organisations, public institutions and foreign partners within the scope of the group’s goals (§ 3(5), § 23(1)).

INSAI Network. The group is building a circle of people and institutions from outside the University of Warsaw with whom it intends to carry out research, share knowledge and run joint projects.

How the group began

Aleksander Rej, president

The group grew out of work I had previously done on my own.

I work on how AI is changing science and academia.

I co-founded INSAI in May 2026 and have been its president since July.

The talks and works listed below are my personal record, not the group’s. The exceptions are the two entries from October 2026 on recruitment and the fair, which concern the group’s activities.

Before the group was founded

  1. 17–18 May 2025

    Zderzenia Poznawcze 2025 (Cognitive Collisions 2025), Faculty of Philosophy, University of Warsaw. I presented the poster “Cognitive collision in education: using explainable AI to develop metacognitive skills”.

    About the conference: Faculty of Philosophy, University of Warsaw, conference announcement

  2. 23 May 2025

    Conference of the Political Psychology Student Research Group, University of Warsaw, “AI in social interactions and voting behaviour”, Prof. Jan Baszkiewicz Auditorium, University of Warsaw. I initiated it and co-organised it.

    The conference programme included: the opening lecture by Prof. Piotr Durka, “Artificial intelligence – truths and myths, hits and misses”; panel I, “Artificial intelligence and cognitive manipulation – foundations, algorithms, social consequences” (Prof. Piotr Durka, Prof. Andrzej Zybertowicz); panel II, “Campaigns, emotions, democracy – how AI affects electoral processes” (Dr hab. Dominik Batorski, Prof. Michał Bilewicz, Wojciech Bednaruk, Karolina Augustyniak-Brzezińska).

    Conference programme: conference website

  3. 27–28 November 2025

    16th National Methodological Conference of Media Scholars (XVI Ogólnopolska Konferencja Metodologiczna Medioznawców), Laboratory of Media Studies, University of Warsaw. I gave the paper “A methodical assessment of AI manipulation risks in the media: an LLM audit study” in the panel “Ethics, law and professional practice”.

    Conference programme: conference website and programme

  4. 3 December 2025

    VII Our Future Forum, Palace of Culture and Science, Warsaw. I gave the paper “Architecture of Access: An Operational Framework for Measuring Embodied Consciousness” in the final of the competition for the best paper and scientific poster.

    Project registration: Open Science Framework

  5. 29 April 2026

    “Człowiek a sztuczna inteligencja” (Humans and artificial intelligence), university-wide course, Faculty of Law and Administration, University of Warsaw. I gave the guest lecture “The sovereignty of science”.

    Course description: University of Warsaw ECTS catalogue

After the group was founded

  1. 14 June 2026

    4th “Science for Society” Congress (Nauka dla Społeczeństwa), University of Warsaw. I moderated the debate “SCI-AI – how academia regains its self-correcting mechanisms”. There I presented a prototype tool that operationalises and extends the Predation Index (Hinze, Stockemer and Reidy, PS: Political Science & Politics, 2026), a method for assessing predatory journals.

    Sources: article by the Congress organiser, University of Warsaw report

  2. 26 October 2026

    Recruitment to the group. The group opened recruitment and published its application form.

  3. 27 October 2026

    Student Research Groups Fair, Auditorium Building, Faculty of Political Science and International Studies (WNPiSM). The group had a stand in front of the Baszkiewicz Auditorium.
